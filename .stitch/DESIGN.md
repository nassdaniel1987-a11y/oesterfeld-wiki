## Rules

- The wiki is a work surface, not a marketing page.
- Keep radii disciplined at 6-8px.
- Use fine borders and tonal layering instead of decorative graphics.
- Do not use neon, gradients, blobs, oversized hero cards, or one-note color fields.
- On the homepage, prioritize search, quick actions, and clear orientation over graph/sidebar density.
- On article pages, prioritize reading width, table of contents, callouts, and backlinks.
